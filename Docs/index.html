1 What is PoriFyx Widget?

PoriFyx Widget is a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform you install on your own web server. Once running, you and your customers can create smart AI chat bots that:

  • Answer questions instantly — at any time of day — without a human being present
  • Collect visitor contact details (name, email, phone, budget) and turn them into scored leads
  • Hand conversations to a live human agent when the visitor needs personal attention
  • Reach visitors across 10 different channels: your website, WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, Slack, Discord, SMS, Twitter/X, and Email
  • Learn from your own content — PDFs, web pages, FAQs — so every answer is accurate to your business
  • Show you rich analytics on who is visiting, what they are asking, and how well your AI is performing
💡

Think of it like this: It is a full call-centre brain that lives on your website, never sleeps, never forgets, scores every lead automatically, and passes hot prospects straight to your sales team — all without hiring a single extra person.
