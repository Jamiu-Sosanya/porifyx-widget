=== PoriFyx Widget ===
Requires at least: 6.0
Requires PHP: 7.4
Stable tag: 1.0.0
License: GPLv2 or later

Adds your PoriFyx AI chat widget to every page and keeps its knowledge in sync with your posts, pages and WooCommerce products.

== Installation ==
1. Upload the plugin zip in Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin, then activate it.
2. Go to Settings > PoriFyx Widget.
3. Enter your platform URL, Widget ID (Widget > Embed code) and Widget token (Widget > Security).
4. Save, then click "Sync now" to send existing content. New and edited content syncs automatically.

== Notes ==
* Page builders (Elementor, Divi, Beaver Builder and others) are supported: content is rendered before it is sent.
* Unpublished, password-protected and deleted items are removed from the assistant's knowledge.
* Sync runs in batches of 25, so it works on shared hosting.
