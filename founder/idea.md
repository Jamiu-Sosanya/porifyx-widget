# PoriFyx Widget

- What it is: A SaaS AI chat assistant that businesses add to their website with one script tag. It is trained on the business's own web pages, PDFs and question-and-answer pairs, answers visitors, captures and scores leads, and hands conversations to human staff.
- Who it is for: Small and mid-sized businesses (roughly 5 to 50 staff), including e-commerce shops, clinics and service businesses, SaaS companies, and agencies that run assistants for several clients.
- What it sells, at what price: Monthly or yearly subscriptions in USD (Stripe card, PayPal or bank transfer). Free plan forever: 1 widget, 100 AI replies/month. Starter $19/mo ($190/yr, 2,000 AI replies), Pro $49/mo ($490/yr, 8,000), Business $129/mo ($1,290/yr, 30,000), Enterprise $299/mo ($2,990/yr, unlimited). AI is included in every plan: no per-reply credits, no automatic upgrades. Optional AI reply top-ups that never expire: 1,000 for $5, 5,000 for $19, 20,000 for $59. New sign-ups get a 14-day Pro trial with no card, then drop to Free instead of switching off.
- Where and how: Online and sold worldwide from porifyx.com. How customers will find it has not been decided yet. Delivered as hosted software; the app is a Laravel application that can also run on ordinary shared hosting.
- Budget and constraints: Solo founder (Jamiu Sosanya), who also runs other products under the PoriFyx brand. Budget not stated. Pre-launch, with no customers yet. The codebase began as a script bought on the Codester marketplace and has been fully rebranded and extended.
- Product today: AI chat trained on site pages, PDFs and Q&A, with a confidence threshold and fallback reply; lead capture and scoring; shared inbox and human handoff; tickets with auto-escalation; bookings inside the chat with calendar sync; no-code chat flows on a canvas; 21 workflow automation recipes; analytics emailed on a schedule; 133 languages; integrations hub (WordPress plugin, WooCommerce, CRM, calendars, Slack, WhatsApp, MCP); multiple workspaces and custom team roles; GDPR tools.
- Competitor research (founder/competitors.md): rivals Chatbase, Tidio + Lyro, Botsonic, SiteGPT, CustomGPT, Intercom Fin, tawk.to. Top buyer complaints: unpredictable bills (credits, automatic upgrades), AI sold as an add-on, confident wrong answers. Pricing was redesigned against these.
