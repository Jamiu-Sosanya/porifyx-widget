# Buyer panel: PoriFyx Widget: AI chat receptionist that answers questions and books appointments inside the website chat

**2 buy · 18 pass** (10% buy) out of 20 simulated buyers. Seed 699100, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- Proof from a clinic like mine that the chat books appointments my front desk would otherwise miss.
- Seeing it run on my own site with real questions, showing it hands over to my staff instead of guessing, and proof it works alongside Fresha without double bookings.
- A free trial that shows, on my own bookings, that it brings in clients my current booking button misses.
- It writes straight into the booking tool I already use, so there is no double entry and no second calendar to check.
- My web person recommends it and it plugs into the booking system I already pay for, showing me real chats where it booked someone and handed off the rest.
- Seeing it answer real questions from my own price list correctly during the free trial, and hand the tricky ones to my staff.
- Proof from a business like mine, on a real booking calendar, that it answers correctly and that customers actually use it.
- Reviews or a recommendation from a clinic like mine, plus a few weeks on the free plan showing bookings land correctly in my calendar.
- Proof from my own site that chat visitors are booking appointments I'd otherwise lose, shown during a free trial before any card is asked for.
- Seeing it answer about 20 real questions from a client's own site accurately, with no invented answers, during the free trial.
- A written confirmation that it books directly into Fresha, Cliniko and Jobber, plus a setup done for me in under an hour, so I can try the free plan without touching my calendar.
- My web person recommends it, or a clinic like mine has run it for a few months and tells me the bookings are real and the chat doesn't embarrass them.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
