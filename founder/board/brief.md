# PoriFyx Widget

- What it is: A SaaS AI chat assistant that businesses add to their website with one script tag. It is trained on the business's own web pages, PDFs and question-and-answer pairs, answers visitors, captures and scores leads, and hands conversations to human staff.
- Who it is for: Small and mid-sized businesses (roughly 5 to 50 staff), including e-commerce shops, clinics and service businesses, SaaS companies, and agencies that run assistants for several clients.
- What it sells, at what price: Monthly or yearly subscriptions, billed through Stripe, with a 14-day free trial and no card required. Plans as currently configured: Starter 29/month (290/year), Pro 79/month (790/year), Business 199/month (1,990/year), Enterprise 499/month (4,990/year). The currency has not been confirmed; the figures are the numbers in the app's plan settings.
- Where and how: Online and sold worldwide from porifyx.com. How customers will find it has not been decided yet. Delivered as hosted software; the app is a Laravel application that can also run on ordinary shared hosting.
- Budget and constraints: Solo founder (Jamiu Sosanya), who also runs other products under the PoriFyx brand. Budget not stated. Pre-launch, with no customers yet. The codebase began as a script bought on the Codester marketplace and has been fully rebranded and extended.

## Product detail

- AI providers: OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Groq and OpenRouter. Each assistant can use a different model, and a backup provider answers automatically if the main one fails. The business pays for AI usage through API keys configured by the platform operator (whether customers bring their own keys has not been decided).
- Training: website URLs, uploaded documents and Q&A pairs, with saved versions and one-click rollback.
- Answer safety: a "strict mode" that limits answers to the knowledge base and offers a human instead of guessing.
- Leads: contact capture during chats, automatic cold/warm/hot scoring, export to Excel and PDF, webhooks and simple automations to push leads to other systems.
- Team: shared inbox, live human takeover with AI summaries, visitor sentiment and suggested replies, canned responses, offline messages.
- Growth tools: proactive messages (time on page, scroll, exit intent), A/B tests of the widget.
- Channels: website plus WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, X, Slack, Discord, SMS and email from one trained assistant.
- Also built: public marketing site with a live demo assistant, documentation, API reference, admin panel, cookie consent controls, PWA support, two-factor login.
