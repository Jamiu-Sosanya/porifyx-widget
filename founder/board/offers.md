# Offers lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The pain is real. Small businesses miss visitors, leads and questions after hours.
- Effort is low. One script tag, trained on pages and PDFs the buyer already has.
- The stack is deep. Chat, lead scoring, inbox, handoff, bookings, flows and automations come in one product.
- The pricing answers known buyer complaints: AI included, no surprise upgrades, top-ups that never expire.
- The trial asks nothing. No card, and it falls back to Free instead of shutting off.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Likelihood has no proof.** No customers, no case studies, no numbers. Buyers already fear confident wrong answers. Nothing yet shows this one gets it right.
2. **It sells on price and fairness, not outcome.** "Cheaper and more predictable" is a commodity position in a crowded market. The offer names features, not a result like "more booked calls" or "fewer support tickets".
3. **The market is too wide to reach.** Shops, clinics, SaaS and agencies have different dream outcomes. One offer for all of them speaks to none. How buyers will be found is undecided.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Which one segment feels this pain most and pays fastest?
- What result does that buyer get, in numbers, and how fast?
- Why would they believe it works before paying? What proof exists today?
- What is the guarantee? Could the business afford one tied to an outcome?
- Could the price double and still be the obvious choice? If not, why not?
- Is there any real scarcity or urgency, or none?
- How many trial users will see a useful answer in their first session?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- Pick one segment and rewrite the offer around its single dream outcome.
- Get a small set of real users and turn their results into proof before wider launch.
- Add a guarantee tied to an outcome, sized so the business can afford it.
- Lead with the result, not the feature list. Keep the stack as support, not the headline.
- Decide how the first buyers will be reached before spending on anything else.
