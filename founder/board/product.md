# Product lens
Score: 5 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The first touch is simple. One script tag on a site is a clear start.
- Pricing answers real complaints buyers make about rivals: AI in every plan, no surprise upgrades, top-ups that never expire.
- The trial ends gently. It drops to Free, not to a dead widget. That is a well-made seam.
- The confidence threshold and fallback reply go after the worst complaint in this market: confident wrong answers.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No one thing.** Chat, leads, inbox, tickets, bookings, flows, 21 automations, analytics, 133 languages, workspaces, many integrations. The pitch needs a paragraph. A small business will not know what it is buying. The Product lens says that means the product is not clear yet.
2. **Width over depth, done by one person.** A solo founder with other products cannot polish this much surface. Rough edges in bookings or calendar sync will make the whole thing feel cheap. Much of the code came from a bought script, so how well the hidden parts are built is unknown.
3. **Many seams.** Each handoff is a place where it can break: bot to human, chat to calendar, chat to CRM, WhatsApp, Slack. No one has tested how these feel to a real visitor, because there are no customers yet.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Say it in one sentence. What is the one thing it does better than anything else?
- What do the first five minutes feel like for a 10-person clinic owner? How long until the bot gives its first good answer?
- Which half of the feature list would you cut today?
- How do you know the answers are right? What happens when the bot is unsure and no human is online?
- Which buyer type comes first: shops, clinics, SaaS firms or agencies? They need different products.
- How well do you know the code you bought and did not write?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- Pick one buyer type and one promise. Hide or cut every feature that does not serve it.
- Make the first five minutes great: paste a URL, get correct answers, go live. Measure it.
- Test the bot-to-human handoff end to end with real visitors before launch.
- Audit the bought code for quality and security before charging anyone.
