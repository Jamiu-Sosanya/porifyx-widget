# Monopoly lens
Score: 3/10 | Vote: PASS

## What is strong
- The product is real and wide: many AI providers with automatic backup, strict mode, lead scoring, human takeover and ten channels from one trained assistant. Building it cost little because it started from a bought script.
- The costs are low. It runs on cheap shared hosting, so a small, focused market could still make money.
- The agency use case (one person running assistants for several clients) is a possible small market to own first, because each agency brings many end customers.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Anyone can copy it.** The code started as a script that anyone can buy on Codester. A rival could buy the same base for a few dollars, and nothing in the brief shows proprietary technology, network effects, scale or brand. Nothing stops a copycat in month six. The lens's guess is that nothing will.
2. **It is a crowded category with no small market chosen.** "Small and mid-sized businesses, e-commerce, clinics, SaaS, agencies, worldwide" is a broad market, not a small one it can own first. The lens expects many existing AI chat widgets aimed at the same buyer. How many there are and what they charge is not known yet (/founder-competitors answers this).
3. **There is no distribution plan.** How customers will find it has not been decided. The founder works alone and splits time across other PoriFyx products. The cost to win each customer is unknown (/founder-cfo, once /founder-consumer gives buy rates).

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What can this do at least ten times better than the next option, and for which single type of business?
- What do you know about that buyer that rivals do not? This is your secret.
- Which small market could you own most of within a year? For example, clinics in one country, or agencies that resell chat.
- How do the first 100 customers arrive, and what does each one cost?
- Why now? What has changed that makes this the right moment?
- What does one conversation cost in AI usage, and does the Starter plan at 29 a month still earn a margin? (/founder-cfo)
- Who else sells this within reach of the same buyer, and at what price? (/founder-competitors)
- Will customers pay, and which segment buys first? (/founder-consumer)

## If FUND IF: the conditions
The vote is PASS, so this section does not apply. The vote would move to FUND IF:
- The founder picks one narrow segment and shows (/founder-competitors) that no one serves it well.
- There is a named, costed route to the first 100 customers in that segment.
- There is a credible plan to build one moat, such as data from that segment, integrations rivals lack or a brand within that niche.
