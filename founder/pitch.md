PoriFyx is an AI receptionist for your website chat. It learns from your site pages, PDFs and price list, answers visitors' questions any time of day, and books appointments inside the chat, synced to Google or Outlook calendar.
When it is unsure, it says so and passes the chat to your staff instead of guessing.
Add it with one line of code or the WordPress plugin; most setups take under 15 minutes.
Price: Free plan forever (100 AI replies a month). Starter $19/month (2,000 replies). Pro $49/month (8,000 replies, your branding). AI is included in every plan: no per-reply credits and no automatic upgrades. Extra replies cost $5 per 1,000 and never expire.
Try Pro free for 14 days with no card; after that it drops to the Free plan, not off.
